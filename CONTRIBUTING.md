# Contributing

This repository contains research and recommendations prepared as part of the Expensify Customer Experience Generalist UK final-round process. It is a personal, public record of work, not a product or a project.

That said, feedback is welcome.

## How to give feedback

- Open an [Issue](../../issues/new) for anything substantive: a recommendation you disagree with, a missing consideration, a factual correction, or a different angle on the prioritisation.
- Comment directly on an existing Issue if your point fits there.
- For light typo fixes or small clarity improvements, open a Pull Request directly.

## Tone

The tone of this repo follows what I understand to be Expensify's writing style: direct, friendly, opinion-having, and plain-English. Feedback in the same register is appreciated.

## What I will not change without good evidence

- The persona (first-time UK-based individual user) is deliberate. Recommendations are scoped to that lens.
- The illustrative business-impact numbers are flagged as illustrative throughout. They are placeholders for actual analytics work, not a claim of precision.
- The original April 2025 PDF in [original-submission](original-submission/) is archived as-submitted and will not be edited.
